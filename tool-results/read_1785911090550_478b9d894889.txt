   363→### Key findings
   364→1. **libkr64.so is NOT a JNI library** — it's a standalone ELF executable disguised as .so, launched
   365→   by the Android kernel via the custom libkrloader64.so interpreter (PT_INTERP). Its entry point
   366→   is _start at 0x4db0 → __libc_init → main() at 0x7244.
   367→
   368→2. **libkr64.so has 187 imported symbols** (not 3). Full list includes bind, socket, listen,
   369→   accept4, socketpair, connect, fork, clone, mknodat, mkdirat, symlinkat, linkat, ioctl, mmap,

   373→   getnameinfo, etc. NO exported FUNC symbols at all (completely stripped).
   374→

   376→   libkr64.so are all for NETLINK emulation (netlink_server, nl_dhcp_%d_%d, netdevice_%d_%d),

   378→   /vm%d/dev/binder is correct — libkr64.so creates everything ELSE.
   379→

   381→   dynamic linker's __dl__Z9do_dlopenPKciPK17android_dlextinfo function. This lets libkr64.so
   382→   intercept every dlopen() call the guest makes and redirect it.
   383→
   384→5. **libkr64.so embeds LZMA/XZ + zlib 1.2.8 decompressors** — for decompressing embedded
   385→   configuration data at runtime.
   386→
   387→6. **libkr64.so installs a seccomp filter** on the guest with a SIGSYS handler that emulates
   388→   "blocked" syscalls. Strings: "init_seccomp", "__NR_rt_sigaction SIGSYS", "BLOCKED.SYSCALL.FAILED".
   389→
   390→7. **libkr64.so manages a per-VM mount namespace** (vm.mount.ns) with bind mounts, tmpfs, and
   391→   propagation control. Skips /dev, /mnt, /storage as "special".
   392→
   393→8. **libkr64.so creates 20+ virtual device files** via mknodat: /dev/vmproc, /dev/__kmsg__,
   394→   /dev/__kmsg2__, /dev/__krlog__, /dev/__properties__, /dev/ashmem, /dev/ashmemsim, /dev/tmpfs,
   395→   /dev/.busybox, /dev/.coldboot_done, /dev/socket/process_pid, /dev/socket/logdw,
   396→   /dev/socket/logdr, /dev/input/touch, /dev/qemu_pipe (Android 7), /dev/goldfish_pipe (Android 11),
   397→   /dev/gb + /dev/gb2 (Android 11 only!), /dev/block/vdc (Android 11), /dev/fuse (Android 11),
   398→   /dev/hal/power_supply%s (Android 11).
   399→
   400→9. **libkrloader64.so is a custom dynamic linker** built from AOSP source (product marlin/Pixel XL).
   401→   It embeds a static bionic libc. When the kernel exec's libkr64.so, it reads PT_INTERP and
   402→   exec's libkrloader64.so, which then loads libkr64.so and jumps to its _start.
   403→

   412→

   414→    to bypass its own shadowhook hooks. This is the classic shadowhook pattern: hook the libc
   415→    wrapper, but use direct syscalls internally.
